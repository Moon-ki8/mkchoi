This project covers earlier work on transport in soft and bio-inspired membrane systems.
The common thread is the use of molecular simulation to understand how nanoscale structure controls permeability, insertion processes, and channel formation.

The systems range from lipid-wrapped carbon nanotubes to aquaporin-inspired and membrane-based transport platforms.
Even in these softer materials problems, the same multiscale theme appears: local structure and energetics strongly shape the macroscopic response.

## Focus

- Membrane transport in bio-inspired and synthetic systems
- Lipid-wrapped nanotubes as artificial nanochannels
- Molecular origins of permeability and insertion pathways
