This project studies how suspended or partially supported 2D materials deform when their geometry is constrained by patterned substrates.
The main goal is to connect atomistic mechanisms, experimentally visible strain patterns, and continuum models that remain predictive at larger scales.

I use this problem as a clean setting for multiscale mechanics: atomistic simulations reveal the local response near holes and boundaries, while continuum and isogeometric formulations make it possible to study system-scale behavior with interpretable constitutive structure.

## Focus

- Atomistically informed constitutive descriptions for suspended 2D sheets
- Mechanics of patterned deformation over holes and boundaries
- Continuum formulations that stay faithful to nanoscale simulation data
