This project extends materials mechanics beyond isolated systems by looking at open environments, reactive conditions, and stochastic evolution.
The aim is to understand how exchange with the environment changes the language of mechanics and statistical description.

Current work spans statistical mechanics for 2D materials coupled to reservoirs and kinetic analyses of hyperthermal oxidation processes.
Both directions emphasize physically interpretable reduced descriptions for complex atomistic behavior.

## Focus

- Open-system statistical mechanics for low-dimensional materials
- Reactive kinetics and time-series analysis for oxidation problems
- Reduced models for nonequilibrium materials behavior
