This project develops machine-learning models that predict structural and electronic response within one consistent representation.
The focus is on equivariant graph neural networks that remain compatible with mechanics intuition instead of treating atomistics and electronics as unrelated tasks.

The broader goal is to build surrogate models that are useful for multiscale workflows, where both lattice deformation and electronic structure matter under strain, defects, or other perturbations.

## Focus

- Equivariant graph neural networks for multiphysics prediction
- Joint modeling of interatomic energetics and electronic structure
- Physics-aware machine learning for strain-tunable 2D materials
