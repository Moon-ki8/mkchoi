This project targets topological-insulator systems where defects, strain, and composition all reshape functional behavior.
The immediate challenge is to build atomistic models that are accurate enough for dislocation studies while still tractable for larger-scale simulations.

I am especially interested in active-learning strategies that let interatomic models improve as defect configurations and new local environments are discovered during simulation.

## Focus

- Active-learning interatomic potential development
- Dislocation mechanics in Bi-Sb topological-insulator systems
- Structure-defect-property coupling in quantum materials
