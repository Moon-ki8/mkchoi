This project focuses on how substrate interactions reshape the structural relaxation of twisted bilayer graphene and how that deformation appears in electron diffraction measurements.
The goal is to connect a mechanics-based description of the moire geometry with observables that matter for experiment.

The work combines reduced mechanical models with atomistic insight so that deformation, symmetry, and diffraction can be interpreted in one framework rather than as disconnected calculations.

## Focus

- Mechanics of moire relaxation under substrate constraints
- Electron-diffraction signatures of deformation in twisted bilayers
- Structure-property links in low-dimensional quantum materials
