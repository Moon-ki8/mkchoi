This project centers on collective motion and transition pathways in biomolecular systems.
It includes normal-mode-based studies of proteins and viral systems as well as accelerated molecular dynamics for rare-event sampling.

These problems sharpened my interest in reduced physical descriptions: even when the system is high-dimensional, low-dimensional collective coordinates can still provide useful mechanistic structure.

## Focus

- Normal-mode descriptions of collective biomolecular motion
- Accelerated dynamics for rare transition pathways
- Mechanistic interpretation of high-dimensional protein dynamics
